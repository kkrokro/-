Frequently Asked

What are the check-in / check-out times?

Check-in starts at 12:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early/late check-in subject to room availability.

Is parking available?

Yes, we offer free, CCTV-monitored secure parking for all in-house guests.

Do you host events?

Absolutely. Our banquet hall can host weddings, corporate meets, and social gatherings for up to 200 guests.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is available at the restaurant from 7–10 AM at ₹150/person. Select room packages include breakfast.
