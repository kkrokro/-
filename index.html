Our Story

A Landmark of Hospitality
in the Heart of Diphu

Nestled near the iconic Kasa Stadium in Diphu, Assam, Hotel Hambi has been welcoming guests with the warmth of traditional Assamese hospitality since 2010. We blend cultural richness with modern comfort.

From a travelling executive to a family on a wedding visit — every guest receives personalised care, clean accommodation, and memorable meals.
