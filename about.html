Who We Are

More Than a Hotel.
A Home in Diphu.

Hotel Hambi was founded in 2010 with one clear vision — to offer the people of Karbi Anglong and visiting guests a place that feels as welcoming as a home, but as comfortable as a premium hotel.

Located near the vibrant Kasa Stadium in the heart of Diphu, our hotel has grown from a small guesthouse to one of the most trusted hospitality destinations in the region. Every room, every meal, and every service is delivered with the spirit of Bihu — joyful, warm, and wholehearted.

We are proud to serve business travellers, families, couples, and event guests — providing tailored experiences that make every visit unforgettable.

Our Location

Perfectly Placed
in Diphu

Hotel Hambi is situated near the iconic Kasa Stadium in Diphu, the headquarters of Karbi Anglong district — making it easily accessible from all parts of the city and district.

Whether you're arriving by road from Guwahati (approx. 4 hours) or by rail at Lumding Junction, our hotel is conveniently reachable and a recognisable landmark.
